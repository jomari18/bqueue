# Credits

The same credits appear on the site's `/credits` page.

- **UI design:** UI adapted from "Donka Help Desk — Internal Request System" by Dayse (Figma Community), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: forest green accent, new brand, barangay screens and content, icons.
- **3D scene:** 3D scene inspired by "Reeded liquid glass – Prism hero section concept" on Spline, rebuilt from primitives; no files from it are used.
- **Fonts:** the families the Donka file uses, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and self-hosted:
  - Sora, by Jonathan Barnbrook and Julián Moncada: headings, the brand and the 3D ticket's numbers (`apps/web/public/fonts/ticket.woff` is Sora 800 from `@fontsource/sora`)
  - Inter, by Rasmus Andersson: body text
  - Noto Serif, by Google: card titles and the display brand
- **Icons:** Lucide, ISC License.
- **Libraries:** three.js, React Three Fiber and drei (MIT) for the 3D ticket; qrcode (MIT) for QR codes; Socket.io (MIT) for the live queue.
- **Sounds:** the TV chime and the phone alert are B.Queue's own, made from sine tones by `tools/make-sounds.mjs`.
